# Enterprise plan

| Valio insight | Demo workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Claims and trust | A signed, time-bound quote captures product origin, price and availability evidence. Recheck stock before order commitments. |
| Approval guardrails | Apply buyer mandates and review thresholds, including recurring quantity and price drift. |
| Partial stock and substitutions | Compile menus from dish names or recipe ingredients. Show per-dish alternatives, coverage and later delivery options. Draft fully sourced menus after checking combined stock. |
| Buyer and Aimo audit | Provide linked inquiry and policy evidence in the order timeline and searchable operations activity. |
| Proactive seasonal selling | Surface time-of-year product suggestions with live stock and price, scoped to buyer constraints. |
| Standing orders | Detect recurring lines using confirmed history. Review changes before confirmation and expose the evidence to buyers and agents. |

Implement and verify recurring controls first, then seasonal discovery, then polish the client workflow. Keep ordering under the existing signed quote, submission and approval paths.

Production rollout requires an Aimo-authorized identity provider, product and order API contracts, inventory reservation rules, merchant-approved seasonality data, operational alert delivery, and an externally anchored signing and audit policy.
